Create a brand new game character splash illustration, using the attached champion image ONLY as a style reference (NOT its character). Standalone transparent cutout, square canvas. Her right hand holds a Poké Ball tightly close to her chest, her left hand lightly presses her temple, shoulders tense. Fully clothed: light oversized hoodie under a dark simple open short jacket, practical dark jeans. Three-quarter figure from top of head to knees, hands and ball fully in frame, generous margin around silhouette. Same high quality BLACK AND WHITE manga ink / crosshatched graphite style as reference, expressive bold clean contours, strong grayscale lighting and detailed but controlled fabric shading. Slight motion in loose hair and jacket, emotional storytelling, no abstract glows, no particles, no background, no text, no symbols behind her, no other characters. True transparent alpha background, not checkerboard. Readable face at mobile size. Keep a sympathetic human presence.
